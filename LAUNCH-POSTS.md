## LinkedIn post (draft)

Transport poverty is easy to describe and hard to picture. So we built a way to explore it.

We've turned 15 real-world use cases from the Equity in Transport course into an interactive site: from gender-sensitive planning in Vienna and cable cars in Medellín, to fare-free transport in Luxembourg and low-emission zones in London.

🔎 Search by keyword (try "women", "heat" or "rural")
🌍 Spin the globe and open a case from its pin
📚 Read each case with its sources
🔗 Jump to related cases that share keywords
